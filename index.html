WHY THIS MATTERS

Empowering consumers across the 35% of care you can plan ahead

Unlike emergency room visits, shoppable healthcare services are scheduled in advance—giving patients real leverage when price information is transparent.

1. Plain-English Procedure Search

Patients shouldn’t need to memorize 5-digit CPT billing codes to understand their care. We translate clinical procedures into everyday language with clear breakdowns of what is included.

2. Cash & Insurance Side-by-Side

Sometimes a provider’s upfront self-pay price is lower than an insured deductible rate. Comparing cash prices alongside payer-negotiated rates helps patients choose the smartest financial option.

3. 100% Independent & Unbiased

We don’t sell patient leads, take referral commissions, or hide prices behind paywalls. Our goal is strictly public-interest transparency so you can compare local options with confidence.

PROJECT ROADMAP

From data aggregation to open consumer search

We are actively collaborating with healthcare data providers and researchers to verify payer and hospital rate files ahead of our public release.

PHASE 1 • IN PROGRESS

Data Partnerships & Teaser Launch

Sourcing and validating hospital machine-readable files (MRFs) and commercial payer rate datasets while gathering feedback from early users and health policy partners.

PHASE 2 • UPCOMING

Public Shoppable Care Search Portal

Launching free ZIP-code lookup across top shoppable procedures—imaging, outpatient surgeries, endoscopies, and lab tests—with facility comparisons.

PHASE 3 • FUTURE

Expanded Coverage & Open Data

Scaling regional coverage and publishing clean, documented summary benchmarks for journalists, researchers, and community health advocates.
